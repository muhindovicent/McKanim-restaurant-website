# DineSight commercial licensing outline (discussion draft)

**This is a product/commercial planning document, not a signed contract or legal advice.**

## Ownership decision (confirmed)
DineSight's product owner retains the reusable software, backend, source code, APIs, architecture and general platform enhancements. The first McKanim/customer installation is a licensed deployment, not a sale or transfer of the platform's intellectual property.

## Buyer deliverables
The contract should specify a configured restaurant workspace, onboarding, approved UGX menu, table QR codes, staff accounts, kitchen/waiter/manager modules, reporting and operational training. AI camera monitoring, full inventory, delivery, SMS campaigns, proprietary source transfer and integration fees are **excluded unless separately agreed**.

## Licensing choices
1. **Hosted subscription (preferred):** recurring monthly/annual access, platform support, backups and updates, priced per organization with a transparent branch or usage allowance.
2. **Fixed-term software license:** licence for a defined deployment/term and support arrangement; no automatic source-code ownership.
3. **Enterprise/on-premise license:** optional, with explicit installation, upgrade, security, backup, support and maintenance responsibilities, likely a separate price.

Never promise lifetime free support for a one-time deployment fee.

## Draft service tiers — features only, no unvalidated prices
| Tier | Target customer | Illustrative entitlement |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | Single small restaurant | One branch, QR menu/orders, kitchen, table workflow, basic reporting |
| Growth | Larger/busier restaurant or small chain | Multiple branches, manager reporting, staff assignments and support options |
| Enterprise | Group requiring custom deployment | Custom branch limits, integrations, formal SLA and advanced governance |
| AI add-on | Suitable locations with approved cameras | Optional table monitoring after privacy/accuracy validation |

Billing rules still require decisions: free trial, cancellation, grace period, overage protection, data export, limits, support severity and payment provider. No subscription charging is implemented.

## Contract clauses to confirm with lawyer
- Definition of platform IP versus client-owned business data and client-supplied materials.
- License grant, permitted users/branches, prohibited copying/reselling, term and renewal.
- Fees, taxes, payment intervals, suspension/grace periods, refund and termination procedures.
- Service level, scheduled maintenance, support response expectations and security incidents.
- Confidentiality, data processing agreements, retention, cross-border hosting and data export.
- Responsibility for equipment, connectivity, cameras and third-party payment services.
- Warranty limits, liability allocation, exit/migration support and disputes.
- Ownership and reusability of paid custom changes, explicitly stated per feature.
- Branding/trademark rights: McKanim/client identity remains client-controlled; DineSight product brand remains product-owner controlled, subject to actual legal registration.

Before marketing under a registered mark, perform a Uganda URSB trademark search and evaluate copyright/trademark registration with qualified counsel.

## Buyer discovery that remains outstanding
- Actual buyer's restaurant and branch count
- Contract model (monthly/annual, or term licence)
- Restaurant operating hours, tables, roles, tax and payment requirements
- Identity and data ownership expectations
- Target commissioning date and support plan
